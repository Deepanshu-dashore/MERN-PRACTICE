# Week 6 - Day 1: Introduction to Databases & MongoDB

## Topics Covered
1. **Database Fundamentals**: What is a Database, SQL (Relational) vs. NoSQL (Non-Relational / Document-based).
2. **MongoDB Ecosystem**:
   - Collections and Documents.
   - BSON (Binary JSON) vs JSON.
   - MongoDB Atlas (Cloud) vs MongoDB Compass (GUI) vs `mongosh` (CLI Shell).
3. **Core CRUD Operations in MongoDB**:
   - `insertOne()` & `insertMany()`
   - `find()` & `findOne()`
   - `updateOne()` & `updateMany()` with `$set`
   - `deleteOne()` & `deleteMany()`

## University Programs Collection Example
Refer to:
- `university_programs.json`: 10 university programs across `IT_CS`, `Management`, and `Agriculture`.
- `queries.js`: Ready-to-execute MongoDB shell commands for inserting, querying, updating, and deleting documents in the `University_Programs` database.
